**Period:** 2023 through early 2026  
**Commission date:** July 2026  
**Classification:** Institutional white paper — evidence-based, multi-narrative  

---

## About This Study

This integrated research mandate synthesizes uploaded forensic datasets, governance analyses, reform proposals, and capture allegations into a single evidence-based report for ENS delegates, stewards, token holders, service providers, and external governance researchers.

**Methodology:** Mixed-methods forensic framework — quantitative ledger analysis, on-chain governance metrics, forum discourse review, stakeholder interview protocols, and RPGF valuation benchmarks.

**Neutrality standard:** No single faction is treated as authoritative. Competing narratives are reconciled explicitly. Allegations of capture are assessed with evidence ratings (High / Medium / Low / Speculative).

## Cross-Cutting Finding (One Sentence)

The ENS DAO possesses a **strong balance sheet and weak control environment** — fiscal capacity exceeds most protocol DAOs, but governance concentration, budget-variance pathologies, and contributor-economy failures create simultaneous risks of capture, talent drain, and legitimacy erosion unless reforms are data-driven, competitively procured, and constitutionally binding.
