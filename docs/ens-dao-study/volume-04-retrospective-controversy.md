# Volume IV — The Retrospective Controversy: MetaGov EP 6.26 vs. Task Force Alternative

**Abstract:** This volume analyzes the highest-stakes governance dispute of late 2025: whether the DAO should fund MetaGov's mixed-methods Retrospective ($125K, four months, election pause) or adopt the Task Force's immediate forensic delivery. It reconstructs the timeline, assesses capture allegations with evidence ratings, and recommends a hybrid procurement model.

When estmcmxci challenged lack of tender, James argued governance proposals should be "opinionated" and Metagov selection was **"direct accreditation"** (forum page 2, #21648).

| Standard | This Procurement | Best Practice |
|----------|------------------|---------------|
| Competitive RFP | **Rejected** | Required for $125K existential audits |
| Incumbent forensic bidder (accessor.eth) | **Excluded** (forum post deletion alleged) | Should receive formal evaluation |
| Sole vendor | Metagov only | Minimum dual-vendor or Red Team |

**Evidence rating — procedural capture:** **High** — no-bid $125K audit with election pause is indefensible on governance hygiene grounds, independent of intent.

## 8. Recommendation — Hybrid Retrospective Model

### 8.1 Do Not Fund EP 6.26 As Written

Procedural failures (no-bid, pause, sole vendor) outweigh benefits. **Reject or amend.**

### 8.2 Adopt Three-Track Hybrid

| Track | Owner | Output | Timeline |
|-------|-------|--------|----------|
| **Track A — Forensic Ledger** | Task Force data → DAO-controlled | Published SQLite + dashboards | **Week 0** |
| **Track B — Qualitative Synthesis** | Competitive RFP (Metagov eligible, not exclusive) | Stakeholder interviews, sentiment report | 8–12 weeks |
| **Track C — Red Team Audit** | Independent adversarial auditor (accessor.eth or other bid) | Minority report on Track A/B findings | Parallel to Track B |

### 8.3 Election Policy

- **Do not pause elections** without separate Social Proposal
- If WG dissolution pending, elect stewards with **explicit 4-month sunset mandate** OR hold **administrator-only** interim elections
- Retro must not bundle structural change with vendor selection

### 8.4 Budget

| Item | Suggested Cap |
|------|---------------|
| Track B (qualitative RFP winner) | ≤$75,000 |
| Track C (Red Team) | ≤$50,000 |
| Track A publication/tooling | ≤$25,000 |
| **Total** | **≤$150,000** — only if Track A data not already DAO-published |

**Savings vs. EP 6.26:** Immediate forensic action; competitive procurement; no election pause.

## 9. Volume IV Conclusions

1. The Retrospective **mandate is legitimate**; the **MetaGov procurement is not**.
2. Capture allegations are **partially substantiated** on procedure and timing; **unproven** as coordinated criminal conspiracy.
3. Task Force data should be **DAO-published immediately** regardless of EP 6.26 outcome.
4. Hybrid model satisfies reform, refinement, and accountability camps without Labs centralization.
